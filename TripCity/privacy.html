Trip City — Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 15, 2026

Trip City is an app that shows nearby Portland-area transit stops and live arrival times for Streetcar, MAX, and Bus. This page explains what data the app uses and how.

Location

Trip City asks for your location, while the app is in use, to find and sort nearby transit stops and to determine how far each stop is from you. Your location is used only on your device to perform this search — it is not sent to us, stored on any server we operate, or shared with any third party. We do not have access to your location.

Transit data

To show stops, routes, and arrival predictions, Trip City sends your general coordinates and requested search radius to TriMet's public transit data API and to a public streetcar vehicle-location service, solely to retrieve nearby stop and arrival information. These requests are not linked to your identity, and we do not receive or retain a copy of them.

Data stored on your device

Your preferences — such as favorited stops, search radius, and display settings — are stored locally on your device using Apple's standard on-device storage (UserDefaults). This data never leaves your device and is not accessible to us.

No accounts, no tracking

Trip City does not require any kind of account creation, does not collect analytics, does not use advertising identifiers, and does not track you across apps or websites.

Third-party services

Trip City displays maps using Apple MapKit and retrieves transit information from TriMet's public developer API and a public streetcar vehicle-tracking service. Your use of these features is subject to Apple's and TriMet's own privacy practices.

Children's privacy

Trip City does not knowingly collect personal information from children. The app does not collect personal information from any user, regardless of age.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the update will be posted on this page with a revised date above.
