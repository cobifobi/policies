Trip City

Trip City is a simple app for quickly finding nearby transit arrivals in Portland, OR.

Usage

  • Open the app to see nearby stops and arrival times for MAX, Bus and Streetcar.
  • Tap an arrival to see its current location and expected route times.
  • Long press a transit stop in the list to favorite it. Favorite stops will appear at the top of the list when in range.
  • Settings allows you to choose your search range, customize which modes of transport you see, and arrange them.

FAQ

Does the app need location access?
Yes; Trip City uses your location to find and sort nearby transit stops. Your location is only used for this purpose. It is only needed while the app is in use, and is never stored or sent anywhere.

I think this arrival time/vehicle location is inaccurate?
Trip City relies on TriMet's public real-time data feeds. On occasion, there are inaccuracies in the live arrival or vehicle position info. If the information is temporarily unavailable, the app will show "Live vehicle location unavailable"; location tracking will resume once the feed updates.

How do I plan my journey/route?
Trip City does not have route planning capabilities, beyond showing an individual vehicle's planned stops and arrival times. This app is intended as a lightweight tool for finding nearby transit arrivals. For route planning, I recommend PDX Bus or your device's maps app.

Can you add the ability to do _____?
Maybe! Very happy to receive improvement suggestions. However, this app is intended to be lightweight and purpose-focused, so future improvements are more likely to skew towards refinements rather than big new pieces of functionality. For a more fully-featured PDX transit app, I highly recommend PDX Bus.
