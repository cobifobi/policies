Trip City — Support

Trip City shows live Portland-area transit stops and arrival predictions for Streetcar, MAX, and Bus.

Contact

Questions, bug reports, or feature requests can be sent to rewards_placers2u@icloud.com. We aim to respond within a few days.

Common questions

Why does the app need my location?
Trip City uses your location, while the app is in use, to find and sort nearby transit stops. Your location is never sent anywhere or stored — it's used only on your device.

Arrival times look off or a stop is missing.
Trip City relies on TriMet's public real-time data feed. Occasionally a vehicle's live position is temporarily unavailable from that feed — the app will show "Live vehicle location unavailable" in that case, and predictions will resume once the feed updates.

How do I change my search radius?
Open Settings from the app and adjust "Search Radius" to 1, 2, or 3 miles.

How do I favorite a stop?
Long-press a stop's name in any list to bring up a Favorite / Remove from Favorites option. Favorited stops appear in their own section at the top of the list.
